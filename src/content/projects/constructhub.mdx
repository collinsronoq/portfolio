## Overview

ConstructHub is a full-stack construction planning platform built to address structural inefficiencies in Kenya’s small- and mid-scale construction sector.

The platform integrates:

- A **location-aware cost estimation engine**
- A **verified technician directory**
- A **verified vendor directory**
- A **material marketplace with vendor-managed pricing**
- An **AI-powered construction assistant**
- A unified project dashboard

Rather than acting as a generic cost calculator or a basic marketplace, ConstructHub functions as a **construction intelligence system** tailored to Kenya’s pricing volatility, informal procurement workflows, and limited transparency in service provision.

<Callout title="Core Thesis" variant="info">
Kenyan construction inefficiencies are not caused by lack of effort, they are caused by fragmented information systems. ConstructHub consolidates cost data, sourcing, and guidance into a single structured workflow.
</Callout>

## The Problem

Construction in Kenya, particularly for first-time builders and small developers, is characterized by systemic friction.

### 1. Budgeting Without Reliable Data

Most builders rely on:
- Contractor verbal estimates
- Generic “cost per square meter” formulas
- Outdated Excel sheets
- Word-of-mouth pricing

This leads to:

- Underestimated project costs  
- Budget overruns mid-construction  
- Abandoned or stalled projects  
- High exposure to price manipulation  

Material prices fluctuate frequently by region (e.g., Syokimau vs. Nakuru), yet most estimation tools assume static pricing.

There is no standardized, accessible, region-aware estimation engine for small-scale builders.

---

### 2. Fragmented Material Sourcing

Material procurement is largely manual:

- Visiting multiple hardware stores physically  
- Calling vendors individually  
- No structured price comparison  
- No centralized listing of suppliers  

Challenges include:

- Price opacity  
- Inconsistent quality  
- Lack of stock visibility  
- Limited trust in online sellers  

General marketplaces are not construction-specific and lack integration with budgeting tools.

Builders calculate budgets in one place and source materials in another, creating workflow fragmentation.

---

### 3. Technician Discovery and Verification Gaps

Builders struggle to:

- Verify technician credentials (EBK, NCA, EPRA numbers)
- Assess reliability beyond word-of-mouth
- Compare professionals by specialization and region

Global platforms designed for mature, standardized markets do not support localized Kenyan workflows.

The result:
- Hiring based on proximity rather than qualification  
- Increased risk of substandard work  
- No centralized professional directory for small-scale builds  

---

### 4. Knowledge Gap for First-Time Builders

New homeowners frequently lack clarity on:

- Quantity calculations (cement, sand, ballast)
- Permit requirements by county
- Cost breakdown logic (materials vs. labor vs. transport)
- Order of construction phases

Without professional guidance, decision-making is reactive rather than structured.

This knowledge gap increases dependency on contractors, limiting cost transparency.

---

## The Solution

ConstructHub addresses these issues through an **integrated, role-aware architecture**.

Instead of offering isolated tools, it connects estimation, sourcing, and advisory workflows into a single system.

---

### 1. Location-Aware Cost Estimation Engine

- Combines user project inputs (house size, type, materials)
- Integrates vendor-managed pricing
- Breaks costs into:
  - Materials
  - Labor
  - Transport
- Produces structured summaries
- Designed around Kenyan construction phases

Unlike static calculators, pricing reflects **real vendor entries**, not abstract averages.

---

### 2. Material Marketplace with Structured Vendor Profiles

Vendors:

- Upload material listings
- Update pricing
- Manage stock visibility
- Display contact information

Builders:

- Compare prices by region
- Filter by material type
- Align sourcing directly with their budget outputs

This eliminates workflow disconnection between planning and procurement.

---

### 3. Technician Directory and Verification System

Technicians:

- Submit credentials for review
- Maintain professional profiles
- Indicate specialization and availability

Builders:

- Filter by region and expertise
- View verified credentials
- Access structured professional listings

The system emphasizes credibility and traceability.

---

### 4. AI Construction Assistant

The AI module provides:

- Context-aware budgeting explanations  
- Material quantity guidance  
- Regulatory clarification prompts  
- Role-sensitive responses (builder vs technician)  

It bridges the advisory gap without replacing licensed professionals.

The assistant is designed as a decision-support system, not a legal authority.

## Architectural Approach

ConstructHub follows a modular, layered design.

**Frontend**
- React + TypeScript
- Role-based dashboards
- Structured UI workflows

**Backend**
- FastAPI
- JWT authentication
- Domain-based module separation

**Database**
- SQLAlchemy ORM
- PostgreSQL (production) / SQLite (development)
- Role-linked relational modeling

**Core Modules**
- Authentication
- Estimation
- Marketplace
- Technicians
- Content / Knowledge
- AI Assistant

The system prioritizes:
- Separation of concerns  
- Data normalization  
- Extensibility for future integrations
